This website

This website has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or ads. It does not ask for any personal information.

Like every website, our hosting provider receives technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you open. The host uses it to deliver the pages and protect against abuse, and keeps it only for a short time. We do not use it to identify you.

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and we delete them when they are no longer needed.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request about your data: support@playgemwick.site. We reply within 30 days.
