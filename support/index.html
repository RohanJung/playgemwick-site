Please tell us the game, your phone model and Android version, and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

I lost my progress. Can you restore it?

Progress is saved only on your device, so uninstalling the game, clearing its data or switching phones resets it. We don't keep a copy, so we can't restore it.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open the game and tap Ad privacy on the main menu (shown where privacy laws require it). You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's settings under Privacy → Ads.

How do I turn Daily Puzzle reminders on or off?

Solve a Daily Puzzle, then tap Remind me tomorrow (or Daily reminder: On to turn it off). You can also turn off notifications for the game in your phone's settings.

Can I play without ads?

Our games are free and supported by ads. Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose whether to watch one for a rescue.

The game has no sound.

Check the speaker button in the game and your phone's media volume. Some phones also mute games in silent mode.

How do I delete my data?

All game data is on your phone. Go to Settings → Apps → the game → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the game. See the Gemwick privacy policy for details about ad data held by Google.
