What makes it fun

Bomb blocks

Clear a line with a bomb in it and the 3x3 around the bomb blows up too. Plan big chain clears.

Adventure

Level after level of gem hunts that slowly get trickier. Earn up to three stars on each.

Classic

Endless 8x8 puzzle. Chase combos and beat your best score.

Daily Puzzle

One puzzle a day for everyone: easy on Monday, hard on Sunday. Keep your streak going.

Fair pieces

A smart dealer gives you pieces that can usually be placed, so a loss feels like your move, not bad luck.

Plays offline

No account needed. Your progress is saved on your phone.
