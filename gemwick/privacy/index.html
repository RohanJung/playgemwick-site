Gemwick: Block Puzzle: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

The short version

Gemwick has no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress and settings are saved on your own device.

The Android app sends gameplay statistics (for example "a Daily Puzzle was solved in 14 moves") to Google Analytics for Firebase, so we can see which parts of the game are fun or too hard. These do not include your name, email or advertising ID.

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information, such as your advertising ID and IP address, to show and measure those ads. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your choice first, and you can change it later with Ad privacy on the main menu.

1. Who we are

Gemwick ("the game") is made by Dream Big Quest ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Gemwick Android app (package com.dreambigquest.boomblocks), the YouTube Playables version and the browser version. You can reach us at support@playgemwick.site.

2. Information saved on your device

To remember your game, Gemwick saves the following on your device:

  • game progress: scores, best score, Adventure level and stars, daily puzzle streak;
  • settings, such as sound and music on or off, and which tips you have already seen;
  • play-style numbers used to tune difficulty, such as how long sessions last, how often you play again, and whether you left a game early;
  • ad timing records: when an ad was last shown, how many ads were offered, and how many rescues you used.

This information stays on your device. We do not run our own servers for accounts or game saves. It is not linked to your name or email. The Android app also sends some gameplay events to Google, described next.

3. Game statistics in the Android app

The Android app uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service from Google, to tell us how the game is played across all players. It collects:

WhatExamples
Gameplay eventsa game started or ended, mode, level, score, moves, stars, whether a rescue was used, Daily Puzzle streak, ad shown or skipped, theme chosen, reminders on or off
App and device informationfirst open, session length, app version, device model, Android version, language
Approximate locationcountry or region, worked out from your IP address
App instance IDa random ID created for this install of the app, so events from one install can be grouped together

What it does not collect: your name, email, contacts or precise location. We turned off collection of the advertising ID and the use of this data for ad personalisation.

Why: to see which levels are too easy or too hard, how long games last, and whether players come back, so we can make the game better. We see totals and trends, not individual people.

Keeping it: Google Analytics keeps user-level data for the retention period set in our account (currently 2 months) and then keeps only totals. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, resets the app instance ID. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy information.

4. Ads in the Android app

The Android app is free and shows ads using Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). It also uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for consent where the law requires it. These Google services may collect and use:

WhatWhy
Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising IDShowing ads, measuring them, limiting how often you see the same ad, and preventing fraud
IP address, which can show your approximate location (country or city)Choosing ads for your region, applying local privacy rules, preventing fraud
App activity: which ads were shown and tapped, app launchesAd delivery, reporting and analytics
Diagnostics, such as crash logs and performance dataKeeping the ads SDK working

Ads may be personalised (based on your interests) or non-personalised, depending on your choices and the law where you live. Information sent to Google is encrypted in transit. Google handles it under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure

Ad types in the game: optional rewarded ads (watch one to get a rescue), short full-screen ads between games, and a banner. You never have to pay to keep playing.

5. Your ad privacy choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your answer any time with Ad privacy on the main menu.
  • US states with privacy laws (for example California): you can opt out of the use of your information for targeted ads with Ad privacy on the main menu, where shown.
  • Everyone on Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (names vary by phone).

6. Daily Puzzle reminders

After you solve a Daily Puzzle in the Android app, you can choose Remind me tomorrow. The app then schedules a notification on your phone for 7 pm, and Android asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are created on your phone. No data is sent to us for them. Turn them off at any time with the same button after a Daily Puzzle, or in your phone's notification settings.

7. YouTube and web versions

YouTube Playables: when you play inside YouTube, your progress is saved with YouTube's game save service and any ads are run by YouTube. Google's privacy policy applies to that data.

Browser version: progress is kept in your browser's local storage. The browser version does not show ads from us and does not use analytics, cookies or trackers. The website host keeps short-lived server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

8. Android permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and send game statistics. The game itself plays offline.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): only if you turn on Daily Puzzle reminders.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID, added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK): used for ads as described above.

The game does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

9. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Google provides the analytics service to us (section 3) and receives that data to run it. The Google advertising services in section 4 collect information directly from the app. Under some US state laws this kind of ad processing can count as "sharing" for targeted advertising, so you can opt out using the choices in section 4.

10. Keeping and deleting data

  • Game data on your device: it stays until you delete it. Clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app, and it is gone. In a browser, clear the site's data.
  • Game statistics: kept by Google Analytics as described in section 3. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it starts a new, unconnected app instance ID. Because the statistics are not linked to your name or email, we cannot find "your" records from an email request, but you can ask us anyway and we will help where we can.
  • Data held by Google for ads: Google keeps it for the periods in its own policies. You can reset your advertising ID or manage it at My Ad Center.

Because we have no copy of your game saves, we cannot look them up or restore them. If you email us, we only use your email address to reply, and we delete the conversation when it's no longer needed.

11. Children

Gemwick is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has given us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe and the UK, or CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, to object to or limit its use, and to withdraw consent. For data the Google ads services hold, use the choices in section 5 and Google's tools. For anything else, email support@playgemwick.site. We will reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

13. Security

We keep the information we handle to a minimum. Data sent to Google's services is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transfer is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

14. Changes and contact

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change goes live.

Questions: support@playgemwick.site
